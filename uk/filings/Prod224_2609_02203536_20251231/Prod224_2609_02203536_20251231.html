Strategic Report
for the Year Ended 31 December 2025

The director presents his strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2025.

REVIEW OF BUSINESS
The Company recorded a turnover of £21 million for the financial year ended 2025, in line with forecast and representing a year-on-year increase of approximately £1 million, equivalent to a growth rate of 4.2% over the prior period.

This performance is attributable to two principal factors. First, a significant restructuring of the Company's procurement processes has delivered a competitive advantage not previously realised, enabling more favourable purchasing terms, much better relationships and margin management. Second, the Board made a strategic decision to concentrate efforts on core client relationships, shifting from a transactional product-supply model to the delivery of integrated project solutions. Together, these initiatives have created a discernible competitive position in the market and have been the primary drivers of the sales growth achieved during the period.

The improved revenue performance has had a material positive impact on profitability. Operating profit increased by in excess of £500,000 compared with the prior year (2024), representing a positive movement of approximately 190%. The Directors consider this a particularly encouraging result, reflecting both the operational improvements made during the year and the scalability of the business model going forward.

Net assets at the year end stood at £3.33 million, an increase of 0.32% over the prior period, reflecting the retained earnings generated during the year.

PRINCIPAL RISKS AND UNCERTAINTIES
The Company's operations expose it to a number of financial risks in the ordinary course of business, principally credit risk and liquidity risk. Given the size and structure of the Company, the Directors have retained direct responsibility for monitoring and managing financial risk and have not delegated this function to a sub-committee of the Board. The policies established by the Board are implemented by the Company's finance department as required.

1 Credit Risk
The Company has implemented robust policies requiring appropriate due diligence and credit assessment of prospective customers prior to the commencement of trading relationships. These controls are designed to mitigate the risk of financial loss arising from customer default.

2 Liquidity Risk
The Company actively manages its working capital position to ensure that sufficient funds are available to meet operational requirements as they fall due. Decisions regarding medium to long-term debt financing are made by the Board of Directors having regard to the Company's forecast funding requirements and prevailing market conditions.

PRINCIPAL ACTIVITY
The principal activity of the company in the year under review was that of the distribution of specialist electrical equipment to the trade.

FUTURE DEVELOPMENTS
The Board views the outlook for 2026 and beyond with confidence. The Company intends to launch two new commercial verticals during the forthcoming year: a National Accounts division and a dedicated project tendering function, both of which are expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue growth.

Turnover

Intangible assets

Deferred tax
Deferred tax is recognised in respect of all timing differences that have originated but not reversed at the balance sheet date.
