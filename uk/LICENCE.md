# Licence

The company accounts in this repository (`filings/*/*.html` and `pdf/*/*.html`) are Companies House data from its Free
Accounts Data Product (https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_accountsdata.html).

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0:
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

The Tavi files, gaps files, validation files, PDFs and indexes were made from that data by S-Tech Analytics with
Graffiti, and are offered on the same terms, under the Open Government Licence v3.0. They carry no endorsement by
Companies House.
